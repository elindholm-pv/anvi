1. Executive Summary

The DPTF Work Portfolio is performing solidly with 16 active projects representing a total investment of £35.1M in benefits/revenue and £13.2M in capex. The portfolio demonstrates strong strategic alignment with 75% of projects rated Green for overall status. Our focus remains on digital transformation through three core CTO priorities: Core Transformation (5 projects, £11.5M benefits), Digital Platforms (3 projects, £9.4M benefits), and Brilliant at the Basics (5 projects, £9.1M benefits). While the majority of projects are on track, we have three projects flagged Red requiring immediate attention: eQMS Harmonisation & Data Integrity, Legacy HR system UI refresh, and Risk Calculation App Project. These projects face cost overruns totaling £6.5M and require executive intervention. The portfolio is well-positioned to deliver significant value in 2026, with key initiatives supporting our strategic objectives around regulatory compliance, customer engagement, operational efficiency, and technical debt reduction. Critical Q2 2026 deliverables include Stage Gate Project, Risk Calculation App, and NextGen Software Implementation, which collectively represent £5.2M in expected benefits.
